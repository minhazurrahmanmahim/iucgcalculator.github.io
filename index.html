Grading scale

How the numbers are calculated

  • PS (point score) = Credit × Grade Point
  • TPS = sum of all PS
  • EC (earned credits) = credits of courses you passed
  • CGPA = TPS ÷ total credits. By default failed-course credits stay in the divisor, exactly like the IU sample marksheet (53.125 ÷ 20 = 2.66). You can change this on the Courses tab.
  • Remarks come from the CGPA: the highest grade point in the scale that is not above it.
